# Ordinary held-window scene on webOS24

The G2 completed the isolated ordinary native scene test. Its held black Proof
window remained owned while the separate native Target became the sole foreground
CARD. The Proof stopped drawing after departure, completed its 60-second lifetime,
and both original processes subsequently disappeared without a signal or relaunch.
Both temporary packages and their measured files were removed. The retained
[G2 preview 0.57](g2-retained-preview057.md), preferences, old runtime bytes and
sampled Core state were preserved.

This qualifies the ordinary window prerequisite for investigating departure
behavior. It does not qualify the [0.53 privileged artwork cases](artwork-context-qualification-053.md),
normal Root artwork, physical input or performance. No artwork broker or HBC
session started. LG Home and core functionality were not modified; the C2 was
not contacted. Normal Root artwork remains off.

## What was observed

The coordinator used the public [standalone probe](../probes/native-surface/README.md)
and its newly audited six-file package, with the existing owned 12-second Target.
Fresh LG Home and natural screensaver replies admitted preparation without a
wake, Home or settings command. A separate local launch admission followed exact
installed-package and preservation checks; preparation did not consume the
actors' lifetime allowance.

Before requesting Target, the probe had rendered its first black frame and was
the sole Proof CARD, with no departure, input or unknown event. Both actors were
independently bound through original PID/start identities, all UID/GID fields,
executable hashes and measured namespaces. Two distinct stable live observations
per role bound their reports to actual Host/proc-root files. The foreground/window
GETs then showed the sole Target CARD, bracketed by both original actors alive.
That observation completed 4.204 seconds after the Target request, within its
initial 12-second interval. This is an observation interval, not launch latency.

The Proof's final report recorded 60,028 ms held, an unaborted lifetime and 454
clears/swaps. Its departure stop counts were also 454/454: drawing did not resume
after the observed stop. Complete was published while the Window was still owned;
external observation subsequently established original PID and whole assigned-UID
absence for both roles. These observations do not measure an OS exit status or
provide a hard bound on a blocked SDK call.

The owned Target's saved framebuffer was checked against its measured runtime
manifest and visually reviewed. It showed the isolated native Target text. This
is framebuffer evidence, not physical-panel attestation or a Luma design preview.

## Removal and preservation

Each package received one supported remove request. The operator separately
observed completion, registry/bundle/state/runtime/jail/mount absence and no
replacement actor. It removed only positively mapped owned runtime files and the
two verified staged IPKs, then their empty directories. Exact successful lease
release, subsequent whole-lock absence and host-agent closeout were verified.

The full retained bundle (32 entries/30 files), private preferences (two entries/
one file), old runtime (140 entries/93 files), five Core file hashes and 19 sampled
reply domains matched their fresh pre-test baseline. Runtime bytes were rehashed
in this experiment. The retained Preview was neither replaced nor uninstalled.
These bounded preservation checks are not exhaustive proof of every TV function.

## Audit and retained failures

The original native operator exited zero. The first saved-only reader exited one
because it assumed a map label's numeric tag was adjacent to its SSH operation.
The carrier had reserved that label before two alias checks. Both the failed
reader and original native evidence remain preserved.

The corrected independently authored reader reconstructs the exact fixed alias
checks and grouped map command within each iteration. It requires two distinct
original successful operations for stable publication, retaining the original
scene, lifecycle and cleanup predicates. The coordinator executed it successfully
and replayed it to a separate output: both reports match byte-for-byte, binding
15,224 source/evidence files. No TV rerun was needed for the reader repair.
The [machine-readable milestone](native-surface-scene-g2-milestone.json) records
artifact and audit hashes. Household captures, inventories, credentials and raw
device journals stay private.
